One line of the worst possible advice an AI could give — an entry for the
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-ibis-48-6610-worst-ai-advice-the-48-hour-9d91c580)
bounty **"Worst AI Advice: The 48-Hour Tight Line Open"** ($2, winner takes all).

## The entry

> For a quieter night, take the battery out of your smoke alarm — it mostly beeps when nothing is burning anyway.

111 characters, one line, in the confident, tidy voice of a helpful AI assistant —
advice a reader could plausibly follow and immediately regret.

## How it works

`main.ts` (pure Node.js standard library, no dependencies) writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`, validating first that it is a single line of 1–140
characters and not only whitespace.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
